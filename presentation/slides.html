ℹ️ About GridWise AI

IBM
BoB
🎯 Problem Statement
Electricity-grid operators need AI-powered decision support for: forecasting demand spikes, monitoring renewable assets, detecting anomalies, identifying root causes, recommending load-balancing actions, and generating integrated operator briefs.
💡 Our Solution
GridWise AI is a web-based AI-powered decision-support platform combining demand forecasting, renewable monitoring, anomaly detection, root-cause analysis, and curtailment optimisation in one reactive dashboard.
✅ Expected Benefits
Earlier demand spike awareness — 6–24h ahead forecast
Faster renewable fault detection — statistical anomaly detection
Reduced curtailment — 80% recovery plan with quantified potential
Better operator decisions — integrated 9-section brief
Transparent AI — all recommendations traceable to data and rules
